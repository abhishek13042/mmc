# MMC Video 07 — Better Order Blocks; Fair Value Areas

**Series:** Money Making Concepts (MMC) by Arjo Janssens
**Topic:** Fair Value Areas (good vs bad) — the MMC replacement for order blocks / mitigation blocks / breaker blocks

---

## Summary
- **Order blocks, mitigation blocks, and breaker blocks are essentially the same thing — they are fair value areas (FVAs).**
- This is the **overlapping defense** of the order flow lag. Ideal: ODD = fair value area, FLOD = fair value gap. If reversed (FVG is the ODD, FVA is the FLOD) → not high probability.
- **FVAs get created when taking out previous swing points** (short-term highs/lows and intermediate-term highs/lows turn into FVAs once traded through).
- **Ranking FVAs best → worst (based on strength):**
  1. **FVA + overlapping FVG + nested FVA** — three discount/premium arrays = **triple** the probability. The overlapping FVG sits exactly on the swept high; the nested FVA sits just below the original (bigger) FVA.
  2. **FVA + overlapping FVG** (no nested FVA) — still very good; indices show great accuracy here.
  3. **FVA from a swept high/low (the worst)** — when you sweep (wick) the swing point instead of creating an overlapping FVG, the FVA becomes the FLOD and the FVG the ODD → lack of bullish/bearish strength → avoid; often leads to consolidation or reversal.
- **FVG vs FVA entry decision** depends on: (1) what **time** is telling us (volatility), and (2) whether we just took an **intermediate-term** high/low (→ expect a deeper retracement back into the FVA).
- **Speed/fair-value theory:** moving *toward* an ITH/ITL → fast price action, minimal retracements (only into FVGs), because that area is already fair value. After taking the ITH/ITL → slower action, deeper retracements into FVAs (new area to offer fair value). Applies on every timeframe.
- **Case study:** study when FVAs get respected (hold/offer fair value) vs disrespected (seek liquidity).
