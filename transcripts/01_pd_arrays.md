# MMC Video 01 — The 1st thing every trader should know: PD Arrays

**Series:** Money Making Concepts (MMC) by Arjo Janssens
**Topic:** PD Arrays (Premium & Discount Arrays) and how they lead to direction
**Duration:** 15:17
**Date saved:** 2026-06-28

---

## Summary

- **Core principle:** *Price is always moving towards a PD Array.* Our job is to find which PD Array price is moving towards next.
- **PD Array = Premium and Discount array.** Can be used two ways: (1) trade *towards* it (magnet), (2) trade *from* it (reversal/continuation).
  - **Premium array** = bearish PD Array → continue lower from it.
  - **Discount array** = bullish PD Array → continue higher from it.
- **The three PD Arrays (hierarchy):**
  1. **Fair Value Gap (FVG)** — top of the hierarchy. Magnet (trade towards) and trade from. Everything else depends on it.
  2. **Fair Value Area (FVA)** — mainly trade *from* it, not towards. Covered in Video 02. Cannot exist without an FVG.
  3. **Swing Points (liquidity)** — swing highs & swing lows. Trade towards and from. Useless without an FVG.
- Main focus for direction: **Fair Value Gaps + Swing Points**.

## Fair Value Gap (FVG) — definition
- Three-candle pattern:
  1. First candle: can do anything.
  2. Second candle: closes with the **body above the first candle's high** (bullish FVG).
  3. Third candle: does **not** trade back into the first candle's high.
- The gap left behind (first candle high → third candle low) is the FVG.
- Once third candle closes, an FVG either exists or doesn't.
- **Rules:**
  - Mark FVGs on the **daily timeframe**.
  - An FVG can only be **traded into once** — once mitigated, delete it (no longer relevant).
  - Already-traded-into FVGs are not relevant anymore.

## Swing Points (liquidity)
- **Swing high:** 3-candle pattern, middle candle sticks out at top; candle to left and right are lower.
- **Swing low:** middle candle sticks out at bottom; candle to left and right are higher.
- Only confirmed once the **third candle** closes.
- **Why liquidity matters:** big entities need willing counterparties for large orders. Stop losses provide that liquidity.
  - Buy order's stop loss = a sell. Below a swing low = where buy-side stops sit = someone else's buy fuel.
  - Big entities target liquidity to get in AND to take profit (their TP needs willing buyers/sellers).
- **Previous Candle High / Previous Candle Low** = swing points on the timeframe below (fractal understanding). Relevant later in the series.

## Case study (Video 01 homework)
- Mark out **every fair value gap and swing point** on **one single timeframe** (daily recommended).
- Watch how the chart tells you which PD Array price wants to trade into next.
- Works on any market: forex (GBPUSD, EURUSD), indices (E-mini S&P 500, Bank Nifty), crypto.
