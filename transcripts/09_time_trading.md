# MMC Video 09 — Time Trading Made Simple

**Series:** Money Making Concepts (MMC) by Arjo Janssens
**Topic:** Time = Volatility (higher timeframe time / news events)
**Duration:** 20:14

---

## Summary
- **Time = volatility** (how much and how fast price moves). A PD array is highest probability when time supports the idea.
- **Two types of time:**
  - **Higher timeframe time** = the **economic calendar / news events** (this video's focus). Determines whether a higher-TF PD array will hold (enough volatility to actually move).
  - **Lower timeframe time** = sessions / kill zones (covered in the entry video).
- **News is a "smoke screen":** short term it just brings volatility to let your technical analysis play out; long term the news actually sets direction.
- **Economic calendar:** Arjo uses **forexfactory.com**, filtered (yellow folders OFF, CNY OFF), times set to **Eastern / New York local time**.
  - Use it to pick **volatile currencies** (a pair with a red-folder event is higher probability than one with no news — e.g. trade AUD pairs on AUD CPI day; skip NZD if NZD has no news).
  - This supports the **filtering process** trader approach (scan many pairs, pick the highest probability).
- **Always respect risk management** — this is NOT news-trading/gambling (no full-margin NFP plays).
- **Combining time with PD arrays / weekly profile:**
  - Monday no news → consolidation (3rd FVG candle); Tuesday news → sting + continue. → perfect FVG.
  - Monday + Tuesday news → breakaway gaps (no consolidation).
  - Consolidate all week → Friday NFP brings the real move.
- **The big three news events** (wait until *after* release — slippage risk): **NFP** (Non-Farm Employment Change, Friday), **USD FOMC Statement**, **USD CPI**.
  - Day **before** a big-three event → **less** volatile (consolidation).
  - If two big-three events in one week, the **first** won't bring the real volatility — the **last** one will.
- **Psychology angle:** knowing when to trade limits chart time → fewer psychology errors → you outcompete traders who overtrade/revenge-trade.
- **Case study:** study which days a PD array holds and whether a news event (higher-TF time) supported the idea.
