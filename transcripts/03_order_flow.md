# MMC Video 03 — Do yourself a favor; learn Order Flow

**Series:** Money Making Concepts (MMC) by Arjo Janssens
**Topic:** Order Flow (finishing market structure → order flow lags)
**Duration:** ~20:18

---

## Summary
- Finishes **market structure** (short-term highs/lows) and introduces **Order Flow**.
- **Short-Term High (STH):** a swing high **followed by an FVG**. Does NOT need lower swing highs either side (that's the difference vs an ITH).
- **Short-Term Low (STL):** a swing low **followed by an FVG**.
  - In a bearish trend, STHs are protected until price reaches an **ITL**.
  - In a bullish trend, STLs are protected until price reaches an **ITH**.
- **Fractal nesting:** an STH on one timeframe = an ITH on the timeframe below; an ITH on the 4H = an STH on the daily. Same structure, different timeframe.
- **Order Flow Lag** = swing point + FVG + fair value area (all three PD arrays). The FVG is what makes it a lag; no FVG = no order flow lag.
  - **Bearish lag:** swing high → FVG. **Bullish lag:** swing low → FVG.
- **FVGs are superior:** no FVG → no order flow lag → no fair value area. Order flow shows the *intention of price* (the "footprints"); the FVG is the core because institutions can't hide their orders.
- Higher timeframe = stronger. Work from the daily down.
- **Case study:** mark daily order flow lags and see how they tell you the intention/direction of price.
