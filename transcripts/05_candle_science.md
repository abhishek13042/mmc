---

## Summary
- **Candle Science = order flow seen through a single candle on a higher timeframe.** A daily order flow lag is one candle on the weekly/monthly.
- **Why:** the fewer candles you must predict, the higher your accuracy. Predicting the next *one* monthly candle = predicting the next ~22 daily candles.
- Two candle types:
  - **Disrespect candle:** one-sided order flow lags (only bullish OR only bearish). Bullish = up candle with small wick at top; bearish = down candle with small wick at bottom. → continue in the direction of the disrespect candle.
  - **Respect candle:** order flow lags on *both* sides (reversal inside the candle). Bullish respect = long wick at the **bottom**; bearish respect = long wick at the **top**. → continue in the direction of the respect (i.e., away from the wick).
- Respect candles form **at PD arrays** (wicking a swing point / FVG / FVA) → signals the PD array is being respected.
- It's fractal: 1m order flow → 15m candle science; 15m order flow → 4H candle science, etc.
- **Disclaimer:** only combine with the PD arrays taught in MMC — mixing random PD arrays destroys accuracy.
- **Top-down example (swing→day trader):** monthly & weekly = candle science; daily = order flow; 1H & 4H = market structure.
- **Case study:** do a top-down to form your bias and watch it play out over the coming days.
