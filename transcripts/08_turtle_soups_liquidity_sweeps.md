# MMC Video 08 — How to trade Turtle Soups (Liquidity Sweeps)

**Series:** Money Making Concepts (MMC) by Arjo Janssens
**Topic:** Liquidity Sweeps (Turtle Soups) vs Liquidity Runs — Last Line of Defense
**Duration:** 22:08

---

## Summary
- **Origin:** "Turtle soup" comes from turtle traders (trend/breakout traders). They place a **buy stop above a high** (assuming continuation) with **stop loss below recent swing lows**. When price trades above the high but then sweeps and reverses, those breakouts get trapped → "turtle soup."
- **Liquidity = swing points** (swing high = liquidity, swing low = liquidity; also stop-loss clusters).
- **Liquidity Sweep:** trade above a swing high → immediately reverse lower (or below a low → reverse up). Fast, aggressive. The market needed that liquidity to fill large opposing orders.
- **Liquidity Run:** trade above a swing high → keep going higher (continuation). No aggressive reversal.
- **"Comfortable above a high/below a low" analogy:** a sweep is NOT comfortable — immediate aggressive move back (seen via FVG/expansion candle). A run IS comfortable — price continues.
- **Why it works:** large entities need opposing liquidity. Trapped breakout traders' stops become the institution's take-profit fuel.
- **Last Line of Defense (LOD) context (best → worst):** ideally **no FVG in the lag** (just a swing point to sweep & continue). An order flow lag *with* an FVG is more likely to **run** than sweep its LOD → trade towards it, not from it.
- **Two lenses (fractal):**
  - **Order flow sweep:** an actual swing low/high. FVG/FVA gets mitigated → leaves behind a swing low/high → that swing point is what you sweep (not the mitigated FVG — delete mitigated FVGs from your chart!).
  - **Candle science sweep:** a **previous candle low/high (PCL/PCH)** sweep. On the lower TF it's a swing point / order flow lags going one way then the other (the wick).
- **Key rule:** an FVG / FVA can only be traded into **once**. After it's stung, what matters is the swing point left behind.
- **Case study:** mark order flow sweeps in trends and candle-science sweeps; spot the patterns.
