## Summary
- **Entries are the easiest part** — the hard work (direction, narrative, context) is done on the higher timeframe. If you struggle, it's usually the higher-TF read, not the entry.
- Always look for entries **inside context areas**. Entries are all about **fair value gaps**.
- **Two entry types (both order-flow-based, essentially the same):**
  - **Sharp turn:** FVG **in** to context → FVG **out** of context (manipulation/trap). FVGs don't have to overlap. Faster = better. Stop loss above (bearish) / below (bullish) the most recent order flow lag.
  - **Order flow lags:** lower-TF order flow — two lags (trade back into a premium/discount array and continue). A sharp turn usually forms anyway; **pick one method and master it**.
- **Lower timeframe time = sessions / kill zones** (15m and below). Forex: **2 AM–10 AM New York**. Indices: **9:30 AM–4 PM New York** (equities open → bond close). Use a **volatility calculator** (e.g. marketmilk.babypips.com) — trade when volatility is above average. Time is just volatility, nothing magical.
- **Time frame alignment (the baseline / minimum — you can go higher, not lower):**
  - **Sharp turn:** monthly ctx→daily, weekly→4H, daily→1H, 4H→15m, 1H→5m, 15m→1m. (15m is the lowest context area.)
  - **Order flow:** monthly→4H, weekly→1H, daily→15m, 4H→5m, 1H→1m, 15m→1m.
  - Closer the context TF and entry TF, the **less** confirmation needed.
- **A context area IS a market maker model.** On your entry TF, the target is almost always an intermediate-term high/low (the context target on a different TF). Consolidation at lows → sting context FVG (boundary) → confirmation → continue to target.
- **Simplifying** = turning 10 rules into 1; don't add outside concepts (you'll duplicate something already in the plan and lose accuracy).
- **Trade management:** stop loss above/below an order flow lag; target ~**1:2 RR** (ideally within the context area); break even when there's no reason to return based on order flow. Don't chase huge RR — further target = lower probability; 1:2 pairs better with win rate and protects psychology.
- **Probability ladder:** retrace into FVG > rejection from FVG > entry > 1:2 RR > reaching the ITL > sweeping (vs running) the low. The further your TP, the more you demand from price.
- **Choose your baseline** entry confirmation and add *extra* confirmation (more order flow lags / higher-TF sharp turn) when the context area is lower probability.
