# MMC Video 12 — A Top Down Analysis that suits YOU

**Series:** Money Making Concepts (MMC) by Arjo Janssens
**Topic:** Top-Down Analysis — filtering process vs flow trader (final video)
**Duration:** 17:49

---

## Summary
- **Purpose of a top-down:** find **direction → narrative → a high-probability context area** where you look for entries (entry itself isn't part of the top-down).
- **Choose your own trading style** — which concept on which timeframe depends on the trader you want to be (long-term → monthly market structure/candle science; day trader → daily candle science + 4H order flow; scalper → 1H candle science). More daily time available → go lower TF; less time → higher TF.
- **Two trader paths:**
  - **Filtering process trader:** focuses on the **higher timeframe**, scans **many Forex pairs**, uses **arguments** (order flow + market structure + candle science) to pick the single highest-probability pair. One-sided arguments (all bullish, no bearish) = highest probability; mixed = ~50/50. Stops drilling at the 4H (only goes to 4H if already in daily/weekly/monthly context). Lower trade frequency (~1 great trade/week).
  - **Flow trader:** focuses on the **lower timeframe** (down to 1m), one **single instrument** — ideally the **most volatile** (indices: ES, NASDAQ, YM, Russell). More reactive, "ping-pong" bullish/bearish, guided by 15m & 1H FVGs toward higher-TF PD arrays. Higher trade frequency (potentially one high-prob trade/day; ~3/week is good).
- Neither is better — suits different life stages (busy → filtering process; more capital → flow trading loses appeal, go up TFs).
- **Master one method first** before switching.
- **Downfalls / advice:**
  - **Don't replay-backtest** (it builds bad habits — 5 clicks ≠ 5 days; trading needs patience). Hindsight backtesting (training your eyes) is fine.
  - **Learn from your mistakes** — repeating them = endless cycles.
  - **Don't incorporate outside concepts** — they're likely already in the (simplified) plan; adding them loses accuracy.
- Worked example: GBPUSD vs EURUSD top-down — EURUSD stronger (clear respect candles, expansion-phase candles) → higher probability for longs.
- **Case study:** do a full top-down to form your bias and watch it play out over the coming days.
- **End of the MMC series.** Arjo points to the MMT (ario.io) for more.
